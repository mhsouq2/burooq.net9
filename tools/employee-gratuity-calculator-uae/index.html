The UAE gratuity formula

  • First 5 years of service: 21 days' basic salary for each year
  • After 5 years of service: 30 days' basic salary for each additional year
  • Cap: Total gratuity is generally capped at two years' total salary
  • Gratuity is calculated on basic salary only, not including housing, transport or other allowances

An employee generally needs to complete at least one full year of continuous service to be entitled to gratuity. Calculations can vary slightly for early resignation under certain contract types versus termination by the employer, and rules have been updated over time — always verify against current MOHRE guidance for a specific case.

This is an estimate, not legal advice

UAE gratuity rules have specific provisions depending on contract type, reason for leaving, and unpaid leave periods that can affect the final figure. This calculator gives a solid planning estimate using the standard formula — for an exact, legally binding calculation, consult MOHRE guidance or an employment lawyer.
