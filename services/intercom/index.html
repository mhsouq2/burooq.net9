Intercom Systems

Wired video and audio door entry for villas, gates and apartments, integrated with access control and gate motors. Same-day repair for any brand.

Get a Quote for This Service WhatsApp an Engineer

A gate intercom that fails is a security gap, not just an inconvenience — visitors and deliveries end up let in without verification, or turned away entirely. We install wired video intercoms for reliability and repair any brand, any age of system, regardless of who installed it originally, with same-day response for most faults.

Multi-entrance systems

Wired or wireless intercom?

A wired video intercom with a colour touchscreen monitor and mobile app support is the most reliable choice for most villas. Wireless intercoms install faster but rely on WiFi consistency and battery maintenance — the same coverage issues that cause WiFi dead zones can also affect a wireless intercom's reliability. A single-entrance wired video intercom typically costs AED 600–1,200 for equipment, with installation labor on top.

Audio-only faults are usually wiring or speaker issues and are quick to fix. Video intercom faults can also involve the camera module, the monitor's screen, or the network connection on IP-based systems, so diagnosis takes a little longer. We confirm the exact cost before starting any repair.
