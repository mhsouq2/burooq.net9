Booster, mesh or access point — which do you need?

A cheap plug-in booster is the right answer for some homes and the wrong one for many UAE villas. Concrete walls, reinforced floor slabs and long majlis layouts block WiFi far more than the drywall homes these products are designed for. That's why we test the signal first instead of just selling a box.

WiFi booster / extender

Good for one weak room in an apartment or a small villa floor. Cheap and quick, but a wireless extender usually halves the speed it passes on, so it's not ideal for streaming or gaming far from the router.

Mesh WiFi system

Two or three units that act as one network with one name, so phones roam automatically. Works well in apartments and most townhouses, and much better than extenders for whole-home coverage.

Wired access points

The professional fix for large concrete villas and offices: access points connected by cable, giving full speed in every room. If you need this, our WiFi solutions and structured cabling team designs the full system.
