Laptops in the UAE work harder than most

Heat and fine dust are a laptop's biggest enemies, and Abu Dhabi has plenty of both. Dust builds up on the cooling fan and heatsink, the dried-out thermal paste stops moving heat away from the processor, and the laptop starts throttling — getting slow, loud and hot — long before it finally shuts down. Most "my laptop is suddenly slow" calls we get are fixed with a proper internal clean and a switch from an old hard drive to an SSD, not a new laptop.

We tell you honestly whether a repair is worth it. If the repair would cost more than the laptop is worth, we'll say so — and still help you save your files.

Laptop repairs and maintenance we do

Overheating & fan noise

Full strip-down clean, new thermal paste, fan service or replacement. The most common fix for slow, hot, shutting-down laptops.

Slow laptop speed-up

SSD upgrade with full transfer of Windows and your files, RAM upgrade, startup clean-up. Typically feels like a new machine.

Screen replacement

Cracked, lines, flickering or black screens, including touch screens and high-refresh gaming panels.

Battery replacement

Battery draining fast, swollen, or laptop only works on charger. Swollen batteries should be replaced promptly for safety.

Keyboard & touchpad

Missing keys, liquid-damaged keyboards and unresponsive touchpads repaired or replaced.

Windows, software & viruses

Windows reinstall or repair, driver fixes, virus and malware removal, Microsoft Office and email setup.

Won't turn on / no charging

Power faults, charging ports, DC jacks, chargers and board-level faults assessed case by case.

Liquid damage

Spilled tea, water or juice: switch off immediately, don't try to charge it, and bring it in as soon as possible.

Business & office laptops

Multi-laptop maintenance for offices, new laptop setup, backup and data migration — add to an annual maintenance contract.

Brands we repair

HP (Pavilion, EliteBook, ProBook, Victus, Omen), Dell (Inspiron, Latitude, Vostro, XPS, Alienware), Lenovo (IdeaPad, ThinkPad, Legion, Yoga), Asus (VivoBook, ZenBook, ROG, TUF), Acer (Aspire, Nitro, Predator, Swift), MSI, Microsoft Surface, Huawei MateBook and Apple MacBook Air / MacBook Pro.

Gaming laptops

Gaming laptops run hot by design and suffer most from UAE dust. A clean and re-paste every 6–12 months keeps frame rates stable and protects the GPU. We also do SSD and RAM upgrades for gaming machines.

Simple maintenance that saves you money

  • Get an internal clean every 12 months (6 months for gaming laptops or dusty work sites).
  • Don't use the laptop on beds, cushions or carpets — they block the air vents.
  • Never leave a laptop in a parked car in summer; cabin heat damages batteries and screens.
  • Upgrade to an SSD if your laptop still has a spinning hard drive — the cheapest big speed boost available.
  • Back up important files to cloud or an external drive. If a drive fails, our data recovery service can help, but backups are cheaper.
