Dash Cam Installation in Abu Dhabi

Free consultation, same-day fitting. Licensed ELV engineers supply and hardwire front & rear dash cams with parking mode — no dangling cables, no melted cigarette-lighter plugs.

Get a Quote for This Service WhatsApp an Engineer

If you've searched "dash cam installation Abu Dhabi" or "dash cam fitting near me," you're probably weighing a quick cigarette-lighter plug-in against a proper hardwired install. A plug-in kit is fine for a rental or a temporary fix, but it leaves a cable across your windscreen and ties up a socket you'll want for something else. Alburooq's engineers hardwire the camera behind your headliner and A-pillar trim to the fuse box or a parking-mode battery pack, so it looks factory-fitted from day one — and because we're the same licensed ELV team behind your CCTV and access control systems, we hold ourselves to the same standard on a five-minute cable run as on a full villa install.

What's included in a dash cam installation

Camera & mount selection

Front-only or front-and-rear kit matched to your vehicle and how you actually use it, not a one-size-fits-all bundle.

Clean hardwiring

Power routed behind trim panels to the fuse box — no exposed cable across the dash or windscreen.

Parking mode setup

Optional dedicated battery pack so the camera can keep watching while parked without draining your car battery.

Rear camera routing

Cable run along the door sills or roof lining to the rear windscreen for front-and-rear kits.

SD card & app setup

Storage card formatted, loop-recording configured, and the companion app paired to your phone before we finish.

Trim refitted, no damage

Panels are reused, not cut or forced — the install shouldn't be visible unless you know exactly where to look.

Which dash cam setup does your vehicle need?

Most bookings fall into one of three setups. We'll confirm which fits your vehicle and driving pattern during a quick consultation — by phone, WhatsApp photo, or in person.

Setup Best for Why
Front-only, hardwiredDaily commuters, first-time dash cam ownersCovers the most common incident type — front and side collisions — at the lowest cost
Front + rearAnyone who reverses in tight parking, ride-hail/delivery driversEvidence from both directions — rear-end collisions and parking disputes are common claim triggers
Front + rear + parking modeStreet parking, shared basements, fleet vehiclesKeeps recording while parked on a separate battery pack, so scrapes and break-ins aren't missed while you're away

Our installation process

  1. Quick consultation. Tell us your vehicle model and what you want covered (front only, front+rear, parking mode) by phone, WhatsApp or in person — no site survey needed for most bookings.
  2. Camera & kit confirmed. We confirm the camera, cabling and parking-mode option in writing before work starts.
  3. Hardwiring. Trim panels removed carefully, cable routed to the fuse box or battery pack, panels refitted.
  4. Mounting & alignment. Camera(s) positioned for maximum road coverage without blocking your view.
  5. Testing. Recording, loop-overwrite and parking-mode motion trigger checked before we call it done.
  6. App handover. We pair the companion app to your phone and walk you through playback and footage export.

What affects dash cam installation cost in Abu Dhabi

A single front-facing hardwired camera typically runs AED 150-350 fitted; a front-and-rear system with parking mode typically runs AED 350-650. What actually moves the price within those ranges:

  • Front-only vs. front-and-rear — a rear camera adds a second cable run along the cabin, which takes extra labor.
  • Parking-mode battery pack — an added component and a separate installation step versus a simple ignition-fed power tap.
  • Vehicle trim complexity — SUVs and vehicles with more interior panelling take longer to route cable through cleanly than a simple saloon.
  • Camera resolution and brand — higher-resolution front/rear pairs and better low-light sensors cost more per unit.
  • Customer-supplied vs. supplied-and-fitted — we can fit a camera you've already bought; labor-only pricing differs from supply-and-fit.

For a worked example by setup, read our dash cam installation cost guide, or get a firm number with a free consultation.

Common dash cam installation mistakes we get called to fix

  • Cigarette-lighter plugs left dangling — a loose cable across the dash that a passenger's foot eventually catches.
  • Camera mounted too low or off-center — footage misses the exact lane or angle needed after an actual incident.
  • No parking mode on a street-parked car — the one time a scrape happens is usually while the car is parked, not while driving.
  • SD card never formatted for loop recording — the camera fills up and simply stops recording instead of overwriting old footage.
  • Rear camera cable routed loose along the parcel shelf — visible, untidy, and prone to getting snagged when loading the boot.
