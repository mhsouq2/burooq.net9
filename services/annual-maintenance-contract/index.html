Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)

Fixed-cost coverage for CCTV, access control and networking systems, with scheduled servicing and guaranteed response times.

Get a Quote for This Service WhatsApp an Engineer

If you've already had CCTV, access control or networking installed, an AMC is what keeps that system actually working two or three summers from now. Abu Dhabi's heat, dust and coastal humidity are hard on lenses, seals, connectors and hard drives — and most failures don't happen on day one, they happen quietly, months after nobody has looked at the system again. A written contract with named response times replaces the usual pattern of calling around for a repair after something has already stopped recording.

What's included

Scheduled servicing

Quarterly or bi-annual inspections depending on the plan you choose, not a one-time visit.

Guaranteed response

Priority callout times written into the contract, not a verbal promise.

Spare parts coverage

Discounted or included replacement parts, depending on contract tier.

Single point of contact

One number to call for any covered system, regardless of original installer.

Written service reports

A report after every visit covering what was checked, cleaned and adjusted — not just a verbal "all good."

Existing system takeover

We'll inspect and bring systems installed by another company onto an AMC after a free compatibility check.

Comprehensive vs non-comprehensive AMC

Every AMC we write falls into one of two structures. The right one depends on how much surprise-repair risk you want to carry yourself:

Plan Covers You pay separately for
Non-comprehensiveScheduled visits, inspection, cleaning, laborReplacement parts — cameras, cables, hard drives
ComprehensiveEverything above, plus parts and replacements bundled inNothing extra — a failed camera or dead hard drive is replaced at no additional cost

Comprehensive plans cost more upfront but remove the risk of a surprise repair bill. Non-comprehensive plans cost less annually but leave parts billed separately if something fails. We'll recommend a tier based on your system's age and size during the free survey — full pricing detail is in our AMC cost guide.

Systems we cover under one contract

CCTV, access control, intercom, networking and fire alarm systems can all sit under a single AMC with one point of contact, instead of juggling separate maintenance arrangements with different providers for each system:

  • CCTV — cameras, NVR/DVR, storage and remote access
  • Access control — card/biometric readers, controllers, door locks
  • Networking — switches, WiFi access points, structured cabling
  • Intercom — panels, monitors and video door units
  • Fire alarm — panels, detectors, sounders (Civil Defense compliance where applicable)

How an AMC works with us

  1. Free survey & system audit. We inspect every covered system's current condition — including ones we didn't originally install.
  2. Tier selection. Comprehensive or non-comprehensive, with visit frequency matched to your environment and risk tolerance.
  3. Scheduled visits. Cleaning, inspection, firmware updates and function testing on the agreed calendar.
  4. Priority callouts. Faults reported between visits get the response time written into your contract, not a queue.
  5. Written reporting. A report after every visit so you have a documented maintenance history, not just a memory of a technician showing up.
  6. Renewal review. Tiers can be adjusted at renewal if your system has grown, shrunk, or your risk profile has changed.

How often does your system actually need a visit?

Most providers recommend at least one to two visits a year, but Abu Dhabi's climate changes that math. Outdoor cameras and equipment exposed to heat, dust and coastal humidity degrade faster than the same equipment in a mild, clean environment — lenses fog, seals dry out, hard drives run hotter. We generally recommend:

  • Twice-yearly visits for outdoor cameras and exposed equipment
  • Quarterly visits for high-traffic commercial sites, regardless of climate
  • Annual visits as a minimum for indoor-only, low-traffic systems

What we usually find on systems without an AMC

  • A full hard drive that silently stopped recording — sometimes weeks before anyone notices, because the app still shows "connected."
  • A camera slowly losing focus or fogging — footage looks fine at a glance but useless when you actually need to identify someone.
  • Corroded or loose connectors from heat and dust cycling, causing intermittent dropouts that look random until inspected.
  • Firmware left years out of date, closing off features and, in some cases, leaving known security gaps unpatched.
  • No documented maintenance history — a problem if a system failure is ever questioned after an incident, especially for licensed commercial premises.
