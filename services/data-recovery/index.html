First: stop using the device

The single biggest factor in whether your files come back is what happens in the first hour. When a file is deleted or a drive is formatted, the data usually stays on the disk until something new overwrites it. Every new photo, download, update or "free recovery app" you install on that same drive can destroy the files you want back.

Do this now: stop using the drive, don't install recovery software onto it, don't run CHKDSK or "repair" tools, and if a hard drive is clicking or beeping, switch it off — every spin can scratch the platters further. Then call us or WhatsApp a photo of the device and a short description of what happened.

What we recover data from

Laptop & desktop hard drives

HDDs from HP, Dell, Lenovo, Asus, Acer, Toshiba and custom PCs — including drives from laptops that no longer power on.

SSD & NVMe drives

SATA and M.2 NVMe SSDs. Speed matters here: TRIM on SSDs can wipe deleted data quickly, so call early.

External drives & USB sticks

WD, Seagate, Toshiba, Samsung and SanDisk external drives and flash drives that show 'needs formatting' or aren't detected.

Memory cards

SD and microSD cards from cameras, phones, drones and dash cams — corrupted, formatted or 'card error'.

CCTV DVR / NVR footage

Extracting or recovering recorded video from Hikvision, Dahua, UNV, Imou and other recorders — often needed after a break-in or incident.

NAS, RAID & small servers

Synology, QNAP and office servers with failed disks, broken RAID arrays or accidental deletion.

Common situations we fix

Deleted files or emptied Recycle Bin

Usually very recoverable if the drive hasn't been heavily used since. We scan the drive with professional tools on a read-only copy, so the original is never modified.

Drive formatted by mistake or "You need to format the disk"

Windows asking you to format an external drive almost always means the file system is damaged, not that your data is gone. Click Cancel, not Format, and bring it to us.

Laptop won't start, but you need the files

If the laptop's motherboard, screen or Windows has failed, the drive is often fine. We remove it, copy everything to a new drive or USB, and can then repair the laptop too.

Clicking, buzzing or undetected hard drive

This is physical failure. These drives need specialist clean-room work, so we assess them honestly first and tell you the realistic chance of success and cost before anything is opened — no surprises.

CCTV footage needed after an incident

Recorders overwrite old footage automatically, often within 7–30 days. If you need footage for the police or insurance, contact us immediately so we can export it before it's overwritten. See also our CCTV maintenance service.

How our data recovery process works

  1. Call or WhatsApp — tell us the device, what happened and which files matter most.
  2. Diagnosis — we test the device and give you a clear price and success estimate.
  3. You approve — no work starts without your OK on the price.
  4. Recovery — we work from a sector-by-sector copy whenever possible, protecting the original.
  5. Check your files — you see the list of recovered files before you pay for the recovery.
  6. Delivery — files are handed over on a new drive or USB of your choice; temporary copies are securely deleted.

Business data, personal photos and confidential documents are handled privately and never shared or kept after handover.
