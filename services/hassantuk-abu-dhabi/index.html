Hassantuk for Villas in Abu Dhabi: What Every Owner Needs to Know

Every villa in the UAE, existing or new, must have the Hassantuk smart fire alarm since 1 January 2024. Here is what it includes, how to get it in Abu Dhabi, and how to keep your villa ready for inspection.

What is Hassantuk?

Hassantuk (حصنتك) is the UAE's smart fire alarm and monitoring programme for homes, run under the Ministry of Interior and Civil Defence. Smoke detectors in each room and a heat detector in the kitchen are linked to a panel that is monitored around the clock. When a detector goes off, all alarms in the villa sound together and an alert reaches the Hassantuk receiving centre, even when nobody is home.

The UAE Cabinet decided in September 2020 that all residential homes must have fire detectors connected to the Civil Defence monitoring system. After a grace period, installation became mandatory for all existing and under-construction villas from 1 January 2024.

In Abu Dhabi, Abu Dhabi Civil Defence Authority (ADCDA) works with e& to install, operate and manage "Hassantuk for Homes".

What a typical Hassantuk system includes

Fire alarm panel

One panel in the villa that connects all detectors and sends alerts to the monitoring centre.

Smoke detectors

In bedrooms, living rooms, the majlis and corridors. The number depends on the size of your villa.

Heat detector in the kitchen

Kitchens use heat detectors because cooking smoke would cause false alarms.

All alarms sound together

If one detector goes off, every alarm in the villa sounds, so the whole family hears it.

24/7 monitoring

Alerts reach the Hassantuk receiving centre around the clock, even when the villa is empty.

Bakhoor mode

Smoke detectors can be paused for a short time while burning bakhoor or oud, then return automatically.

How to get Hassantuk for your villa in Abu Dhabi

  1. Check the official channels. Visit the Ministry of Interior's Hassantuk website (home.moi.gov.ae) or contact e& for the current plans in Abu Dhabi.
  2. Pick the plan for your villa size. A plan reported for a three-bedroom villa includes one panel, nine wireless smoke detectors and one wireless heat detector, with installation, monitoring and maintenance.
  3. Book the installation with the authorised provider and keep the confirmation documents.
  4. Keep the system working. Do not unplug, cover or remove detectors. Disabling fire alarm systems is a violation that can bring a heavy fine.
