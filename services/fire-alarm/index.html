Smoke vs heat detectors: it depends on the space

Space type Typical detector Why
Offices, retail, corridorsSmoke detectorsFast response to smoke in low-particulate environments
Kitchens, food prep, workshopsHeat detectorsAvoids false alarms from cooking steam, smoke and dust

Kitchens and food-preparation areas typically need heat detectors instead of smoke detectors, plus additional considerations for gas and grease areas. Office spaces are generally more straightforward. We design around your actual use rather than applying one detector type building-wide.

The three stages of Civil Defense approval

  1. Design submission. The system is designed for your specific space and submitted for approval before any physical installation starts.
  2. Installation matching the design. Detectors, sounders and the panel installed exactly as submitted — this is where most rejections happen when installers deviate from the approved plan.
  3. Final inspection. An inspector tests that detectors, sounders and the control panel function correctly, match the design, and that backup power is documented and working.

Timelines vary by project complexity and current processing times, so we don't quote a fixed number of days — but staying tight to these three stages, with no undocumented changes along the way, is what keeps most projects moving without a rejected inspection.
