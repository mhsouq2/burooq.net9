Repair Cost Guide

Most garage door repairs cost between AED 200 and 1,500. Remote or sensor fixes are on the lower end; motor replacement or spring repair are on the higher end due to parts and labor involved.

⚠️ Safety note: garage door springs are under very high tension. A snapped spring or a door that has dropped suddenly should never be handled DIY — call a professional immediately.
