Why switch to a smart lock

A smart lock replaces or supplements your traditional key with fingerprint, PIN code, or phone-app entry. Beyond convenience, the real benefits for a UAE household are:

  • No more lost or copied keys — every household member gets their own PIN or fingerprint, and you can delete access instantly if needed
  • Temporary access for guests, cleaners or contractors — a time-limited PIN that expires automatically, without ever handing over a physical key
  • An access log — see exactly who entered and when, useful for households with domestic staff or teenagers coming home
  • Remote unlock — many WiFi-enabled models let you unlock the door from your phone even when you're not home
  • Auto-lock — the door locks itself automatically, removing the "did I lock the door?" worry entirely

Best lock type by property

Villa main door / gate

Choose a model rated IP65 or higher for outdoor exposure to sun, dust and occasional rain. Fingerprint + PIN combination handles daily family use; keep the key backup somewhere accessible but not obvious.

Apartment front door

Indoor-rated locks are sufficient. App-based remote unlock is especially useful here for deliveries or granting access to a visitor while you're at work.

Rental / Airbnb-style units

Prioritise a model with strong time-limited guest PIN support, so each guest gets a unique code that expires automatically at checkout — no re-keying between guests.

Staff / maid's room door

A simple PIN-only lock is often enough here — lower cost, and PINs can be rotated easily if staff change.

Step-by-step: installing and setting up your smart lock

This covers a typical retrofit installation onto an existing door frame. Full professional installation is also available through our Smart Locks service.

  1. Step 1 — Measure your door

    Check door thickness (most locks fit 35–55mm), backset distance (60mm or 70mm is standard), and confirm your door material — wood, metal and uPVC each need slightly different mounting screws.

  2. Step 2 — Remove the old lock

    Unscrew the existing lock body and latch. Keep the original screws and strike plate as a backup in case you ever need to revert.

  3. Step 3 — Mount the new lock body

    Test-fit the new lock before final tightening. Align the latch precisely with the strike plate — an off-centre latch is the most common cause of a stiff or jamming door after installation.

  4. Step 4 — Power on and set the admin PIN

    Insert the backup batteries first. On first power-up, immediately change the default admin PIN — never leave a factory-default code active, even for a few minutes.

  5. Step 5 — Add users and test

    Register a fingerprint and/or unique PIN for every household member. Test the lock at least 10 times — open, close, auto-lock — before relying on it as your only entry method.

  6. Step 6 — Enable security settings

    Turn on auto-lock, enable tamper/forced-entry alerts if supported, and check the access log daily for the first week to confirm everything is logging correctly.

Expert tips

  • Set a unique PIN per person, not one shared household code — this is what makes the access log actually useful, and lets you revoke one person's access without changing everyone else's PIN.
  • For UAE outdoor doors, check the operating temperature rating, not just the IP rating — direct summer sun can push surface temperatures past 55°C.
  • Replace batteries proactively at the 6-month mark rather than waiting for the low-battery alert, especially on a main entry door.
  • If the lock supports it, enable a silent tamper alert rather than an audible one for entry points where you don't want to warn off an intruder immediately.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the physical key backup — leaves you locked out entirely during a dead battery or firmware glitch.
  • Leaving the default admin PIN active — many default codes are published in the lock's manual and are the first thing tested.
  • One shared PIN for the whole household — defeats the purpose of an access log and makes revoking one person's access impossible without changing everyone's code.
  • Buying an indoor-rated lock for an outdoor villa gate — UAE heat and dust will shorten its lifespan significantly.
  • Not testing the lock thoroughly before relying on it — a handful of test cycles surfaces alignment issues while you still have the old lock's hardware on hand.
