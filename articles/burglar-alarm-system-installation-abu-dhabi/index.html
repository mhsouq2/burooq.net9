What's Actually in a Burglar Alarm System

An alarm system is a set of sensors wired or wirelessly connected to a control panel that detects unauthorized entry and triggers a response — locally (siren) and remotely (phone alert). It's a different job to CCTV, and the strongest setups use both together.

ComponentWhat It Does
Control panelCentral brain of the system; arms/disarms and sends alerts
Door/window contactsTrigger instantly when an entry point is opened while armed
Motion sensors (PIR)Detect movement inside a room after entry
Outdoor siren/strobeLoud, visible deterrent that alerts neighbors and scares off intruders
App/SMS alertsPush notification or call to your phone the moment a sensor trips

Alarm vs. CCTV: Why Most Villas Need Both

A camera tells you what happened. An alarm tells you something is happening right now. If a door contact trips at 2am, you get an alert and a loud siren fires immediately — before an intruder has time to search the property. Pairing this with your existing CCTV system means you get the instant warning and the recorded evidence in one setup. If you already have cameras installed, adding an alarm is usually the single highest-impact upgrade for the lowest cost.

The Installation Process, Step by Step

  1. Site assessment

    Walking the property to identify every entry point — doors, ground-floor windows, and the garage — and deciding sensor placement.

  2. Control panel mounting

    Installing the main panel in a discreet, accessible location, usually near the main entrance or a utility area.

  3. Sensor installation

    Fitting door/window contacts and motion sensors at each identified point.

  4. Siren and wiring

    Mounting the outdoor siren/strobe and running cabling back to the control panel.

  5. App setup

    Connecting the panel to WiFi or a SIM card and linking it to your phone for real-time alerts.

  6. Testing and handover

    Testing every sensor, arming/disarming codes, and alert delivery before final sign-off.
