Types of Door Access Control

TypeBest ForWatch Out For
Card / RFIDOffices with regular staff turnoverCards can be lost, shared, or cloned if low-quality
PIN CodeLow-cost, simple entry pointsCodes must be rotated regularly to stay secure
BiometricHighest-security entry pointsHigher cost per door; backup method still needed

The Installation Process, Step by Step

  1. Site assessment

    Confirming door type, existing wiring, and power availability at the entry point.

  2. Reader/keypad mounting

    Installing the card reader, keypad, or biometric scanner at the door frame.

  3. Electric lock installation

    Fitting the electric strike or magnetic lock mechanism to the door.

  4. Controller wiring

    Connecting the door hardware to the access control panel/controller.

  5. Credential enrollment

    Registering cards, PINs, or fingerprints for each authorized user.

  6. Testing

    Confirming reliable entry/exit and any fire-safety fail-open requirements are met.

Single Door vs. Multi-Door Office Systems

A single door is a straightforward, same-day installation. Multi-door office systems require centralized management software so access can be granted or revoked per person across every door from one place — see our small office access control guide for how this scales for growing teams.

Frequently Asked Questions

A single-door card or PIN system typically costs AED 800-1,800 including installation. Biometric systems run AED 1,200-2,500 per door. Multi-door office systems scale with door count.

A single-door installation typically takes 2-4 hours. Multi-door office systems can take 1-3 days depending on door count and wiring complexity.

Biometric offers the strongest security since credentials can't be shared or lost. Card systems are easiest to manage for larger staff turnover. PIN is the lowest-cost option but weakest if codes aren't rotated regularly.
