Best CCTV Camera Brands in Abu Dhabi (2026): Imou vs UNV vs Hikvision vs Dahua

We compare the 4 CCTV brands actually installed across Abu Dhabi in 2026 — Imou, UNV, Hikvision and Dahua — on price, image quality, app support and warranty.

Written by the Alburooq Smart Solutions Engineering Team

20+ years of combined experience in CCTV, Networking, Smart Home & ELV systems in Abu Dhabi.

Published: 2026-07-03  |  Last Updated: 2026-07-20

Ask five installers in Abu Dhabi which CCTV brand is best and you'll likely get five different answers — because the honest answer depends on what you're actually trying to do. Here's a straight comparison of the four brands we see most often across real installations, not a sponsored ranking.

Imou — best for self-monitored homes and villas

Imou (a Dahua sub-brand) focuses on consumer-friendly WiFi cameras with a strong mobile app, AI human/vehicle detection, and colour night vision across most of its range. It's the easiest option for someone who wants to set up cameras themselves and check footage from their phone without needing a dedicated NVR.

Best for: villas and apartments wanting quick self-install WiFi cameras, or a starting point before scaling up to a full NVR system.

UNV (Uniview) — best for multi-camera professional systems

UNV is a purely professional/B2B brand — no consumer app-first cameras, sold exclusively through authorized distributors and installers. Their NVR301 series delivers genuine 4K HDMI output and ONVIF compatibility, making it a strong choice once you're past 4-6 cameras and want a proper recorder-based system rather than individual WiFi cameras.

Best for: larger villas, compounds or small commercial sites wanting a wired, multi-channel NVR system built to run continuously for years.

Hikvision and Dahua — the established industry standards

Hikvision and Dahua are two of the largest CCTV manufacturers globally, with the broadest range of camera and NVR options at every price point. Both are well represented among Abu Dhabi's licensed distributors and offer similar professional-grade reliability to UNV. The main practical consideration is usually which brand your specific installer has the best support relationship and stock levels for.

Quick comparison

  • Easiest self-install: Imou
  • Best for scaling to a full NVR system: UNV, Hikvision, Dahua
  • Widest local distributor support in the UAE: Hikvision, Dahua, UNV
  • Best consumer app experience: Imou

Frequently asked questions

For most villa and apartment owners, Imou offers the best balance of price, app usability and AI features for self-monitored home use. UNV and Dahua suit properties that want a professional NVR-based system with more channels and longer-term expansion room.

UNV and Hikvision are both major Chinese manufacturers with similar professional-grade build quality, ONVIF compatibility and NVR ecosystems. The practical difference for most Abu Dhabi properties comes down to which brand your installer stocks and supports locally, not a meaningful quality gap.

Unbranded or grey-market cameras often lack real local warranty support, firmware updates, and app reliability. The brands compared here all have established distributor networks in the UAE, meaning actual warranty service if something fails.

Most professional NVRs (including UNV's) are ONVIF-compliant and can work with cameras from other ONVIF-compatible brands, though full feature support (especially AI functions) is usually best when the NVR and cameras are from the same brand.
