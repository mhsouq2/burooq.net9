How to Reduce Standby Power Consumption at Home (Without Unplugging Everything)

The advice to "unplug everything when not in use" is technically correct and practically useless — almost nobody sustains that habit. Here's what actually works long-term.

Automate, Don't Rely on Memory

The realistic, sustainable fix is automation: smart plugs on a schedule or motion trigger, applied to your highest-draw standby devices, so the reduction happens without anyone needing to remember anything.

A Practical Priority Order

  1. Entertainment centers (TV, console, cable box) — typically the single biggest cluster
  2. Home office equipment left on overnight
  3. Kitchen countertop appliances with digital displays/clocks
  4. Chargers in frequently-used rooms (smaller impact, but easy to include on the same schedule)

Smart Power Strips: The Multi-Device Shortcut

For an entertainment center with several components, a single smart power strip cutting power to all of them at once is simpler to manage than individual smart plugs on each device, and achieves the same result with one schedule instead of several.

Verify before assuming: not every device benefits equally from this treatment. A plug-level energy monitor (see our energy monitor guide) tells you definitively which devices in your home are actually worth automating versus which draw a genuinely negligible amount.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will a smart plug damage sensitive electronics by cutting power abruptly?

For most consumer electronics, no — this is functionally similar to unplugging manually. Devices with ongoing processes (like a printer mid-cycle) are worth excluding from aggressive scheduling.

Is standby power a bigger issue in older or newer homes?

It's more about the specific devices than the home's age — newer 'smart' devices with always-on connectivity can sometimes have higher standby draw than expected.

Can I schedule different standby-cutoff times for weekdays vs weekends?

Most smart plug apps support this directly — worth setting up if your household's routine genuinely differs by day.
