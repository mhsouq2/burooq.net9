Why This Order, Specifically

The ranking follows a simple logic: devices targeting the largest expense category (cooling, via the thermostat) and the cheapest-to-fix waste (phantom load, via plugs) rank highest. Devices with real but smaller or longer-term impact (curtains, water heating) rank lower — not because they don't work, but because the payback takes longer to materialize.

Don't buy in this order blindly: this ranking reflects typical households. Your own energy audit (see our DIY audit guide) may reveal your specific home benefits more from a different starting point — the retired-couple case study in our full book is a direct example of this exact situation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I buy the highest-ROI device first regardless of my situation?

It's a reasonable default, but your own energy audit data should override the general ranking if it points elsewhere — see our DIY audit guide.

Do these devices work together, or should I pick just one?

They compound — a smart thermostat plus smart plugs plus motion sensors together deliver more combined savings than any single device alone.

Is there a single 'best' smart home brand for all of these?

No single brand excels at everything — cross-brand coordination via Google Home, Alexa, or Home Assistant is usually more practical than trying to stay within one ecosystem for every device category.
