Hidden Cable Installation

The single most-requested add-on to a basic mount: routing power and HDMI cables inside the wall to a lower outlet, rather than leaving a visible cable bundle hanging below the TV. This requires an in-wall rated cable management kit and, for power specifically, either a low-voltage in-wall power kit or a licensed electrician to add a new outlet behind the TV — cutting corners here is a real fire-code concern, not just an aesthetic one.

What a Proper Installation Includes

  • Wall type assessment (drywall, masonry, or reinforced concrete each need different anchoring)
  • VESA bracket matching to your specific TV model and weight
  • Stud/anchor point verification — critical for larger TVs where a weak mount point is a real safety risk
  • Cable routing and concealment (if requested)
  • Final leveling and cable management at the source and TV end
